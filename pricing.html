Pricing

Priced per seller account, for your volume.

Every account gets every check: settlements, bank and fees reconciled; the FBA ledger and claims; GSTR-1, the 2B match, TDS and TCS; stock planning, price floor and account health. The price depends on your monthly orders, the marketplaces you sell on and how many people work in Clarv, so we quote it for your business rather than print a table.

  • Billed monthly per seller account, in rupees, GST extra. Cancel any month.
  • Reading your own Amazon data is never charged: Amazon's Selling Partner API is free to sellers.
  • Accounting practices serving many sellers get a practice console and a per-seller rate.
  • Your data is yours: export everything as files at any time; deleted within 30 days of a request.
